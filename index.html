From prototypes to 3D reconstruction.

I'm a Computer Engineer with a foundation in hardware systems, now focused on computer vision and 3D reconstruction. I'm drawn to challenges where engineering turns into something you can actually see.

I rebuild real objects from ordinary photographs with Gaussian and Triangle Splatting, owning the whole pipeline: capture, camera calibration with COLMAP, training, and real-time rendering on the GPU.

I learn each field by building something real in it, and I do my best work with a hard technical problem in front of me and the freedom to solve it my way.
